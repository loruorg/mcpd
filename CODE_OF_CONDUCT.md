# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone taking part in mcpd: contributors, maintainers, people
opening issues, and people answering them. It applies in this repository, in
anything hosted alongside it, and in any space where you are representing the
project.

## The standard

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to help,
because they usually are.

What that looks like in practice:

- Review the change, not the person who wrote it. "This drops spans when the
  queue is full" is useful. "This is careless" is not.
- Say what you would accept. A rejection with no route forward wastes the work
  that has already been done.
- Take imperfect English in good faith. People contribute here from everywhere.
- Respect a boundary the first time it is set. "Please stop replying to me"
  ends the thread.
- Accept a decision you disagree with once it has been made and explained. You
  can reopen the question with new information, not with volume.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private. This includes unwanted
  contact after someone has asked you to stop.
- Insults, personal attacks, and comments about someone's identity, background,
  appearance, health or beliefs.
- Sexual attention or imagery anywhere in the project.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
  This covers a home address, an employer, a personal email, and anything you
  pieced together from other sources.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, reopening settled decisions to wear
  people down, or filing issues in bad faith.
- Encouraging or arranging any of the above.

Whether you meant it that way does not settle it. The effect on the person on
the other end is what matters.

## Reporting

Email **hi@mcpd.sh**. Put "Code of conduct" in the subject.

Tell us what happened, where, and roughly when. Links or screenshots help. Say
what you would like to happen, though you are not required to have an answer.

Reports are read by the maintainers at Internet Technology Services LLC. Your
report is kept confidential. We will not share your identity with the person you
reported without asking you first. If a report concerns a maintainer, that
maintainer takes no part in handling it.

You will get an acknowledgement within 3 working days, and an outcome as soon as
we have established the facts. Reporting something in good faith will never be
held against you, even if we conclude that no rule was broken.

## What happens next

The maintainers decide the response, and it depends on what happened and on
whether it has happened before. In rough order:

1. **A private word.** An explanation of what was wrong and what to do instead.
2. **A warning.** A record that a further breach will carry a longer
   consequence, sometimes with a period of no contact with the people involved.
3. **A temporary ban.** No participation in the project for a stated period.
4. **A permanent ban.** For sustained behaviour, for harassment, or for a single
   act severe enough to warrant it.

Comments, commits, issues and pull requests that breach this document may be
edited or removed at any point, with a note saying why.

If you think a decision about you was wrong, reply to the same address within 30
days and say what we got wrong. A maintainer who was not part of the original
decision will look at it where that is possible.

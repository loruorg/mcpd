## What this is, in plain words

mcpd is licensed to everyone under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE). The copyright in the
project is held by **Internet Technology Services LLC**, referred to below as
"ITS".

This Agreement records that you have the right to contribute what you are
contributing, and that you grant the copyright and patent licences needed to ship
it. Read it before you send your first pull request. Four things are worth
stating up front:

- **You keep your work.** You retain all right, title and interest in your
  Contributions. You can use them anywhere else, for anything, for ever.
- **There is no relicensing clause.** ITS distributes your Contributions as part
  of mcpd under Apache-2.0. This Agreement grants no right to put them under
  proprietary or commercial terms, and there is no dual-licensing arrangement.
- **The patent grant is the point.** Apache-2.0 already carries one. Saying it
  again here means every user of mcpd has it in writing from you.
- **It covers your present and future Contributions.** You agree once, not once
  per pull request.

If any of it is unclear, ask at hi@mcpd.sh before you agree.

## How to agree

**As an individual.** Add this line to the description of your first pull
request, with your own name:

> I have read the Contributor License Agreement and I agree to it. Signed,
> `<your full name>` `<your email>`.

**As a corporation.** Send Schedule A, completed, from a company email address
to hi@mcpd.sh, signed by someone authorised to bind the entity. Then each listed
employee adds the line above to their first pull request, naming the entity.

Keep Schedule A current by sending an updated copy. An employee whose account is
associated with the entity is treated as authorised to contribute on its behalf.
